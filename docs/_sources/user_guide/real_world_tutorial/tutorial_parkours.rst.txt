.. admonition:: About this parkour

   Four hands-on tutorials of increasing complexity. Each one introduces new
   force-controlled skills in **pitasc** - from a simple slotted maze up to
   spherical motions on a curved surface.

Tutorial details
================

0 - Simple maze with a cylindrical peg
-------------------------------------

:doc:`tutorial_parkour_0`

This tutorial focuses on a very simple maze, solved by moving a cylindrical peg
from start to finish. The peg is held by a gripper on a manipulator arm, and its
motion is governed by the force feedback that the force-torque sensor receives
from the interaction of the peg with the maze walls.

.. figure:: /_images/real_world_tutorial/parkour_0/p0_example_configuration.jpeg
   :alt: Cylindrical peg and simple maze with guiding slot
   :align: center

   Parts used in tutorial 0: a cylindrical peg and a simple maze with a
   guiding slot.


1 - T-piece insertion into a guided fixture
-------------------------------------------

:doc:`tutorial_parkour_1`

This tutorial focuses on inserting a T-piece into a fixture with guides. The
fixture has two guides, one on each side. One side has chamfered edges and the
other does not, which increases the complexity of the operation.

.. figure:: /_images/real_world_tutorial/parkour_1/p1_example_configuration.png
   :alt: T-piece and fixture with guides on the top and bottom side
   :align: center

   Parts used in tutorial 1: a T-piece and a fixture with guides on the top
   and bottom side.


2 - Multi-section maze with door opening
----------------------------------------

:doc:`tutorial_parkour_2`

This tutorial focuses on opening a door by estimating the hinge position and
axis of rotation from force feedback. After opening the door, a maze is solved
using the force feedback observed during interaction between a cylindrical peg
and the maze.

.. figure:: /_images/real_world_tutorial/parkour_2/p2_example_configuration.png
   :alt: Cylindrical peg and a complex maze with door and guiding slot
   :align: center

   Parts used in tutorial 2: a cylindrical peg and a fixture with a door and a
   maze with guides to direct the peg from start to finish.
