.. _guided-insertion:

==========================
Guided Insertion Operation
==========================

.. admonition:: What you will learn
   :class: tip

   How to use several of pitasc's most common skills, including **linear
   movements**, a **guarded approach** and different ways of using the
   **slide** skill. The application focus is the **insertion of a T-piece
   into a fixture with guides**.

.. rst-class:: tutorial-meta

:bdg-primary:`Level: intermediate` :bdg-secondary:`insertion`
:bdg-secondary:`force control` :bdg-info:`chamfer vs. no chamfer`

   :local:

Requirements
============

Hardware
--------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 70

   * - Item
     - Details
   * - 3D printer
     - For the parts listed in :ref:`p1-models`
   * - Fasteners
     - Screws ISO 4762 – M3x35 and nuts ISO 4032 – M3 (or comparable),
       **7 pcs each**
   * - Robot
     - Any pitasc-compatible robot with a force/torque sensor.
       This example uses a **UR5e**.
   * - Table *(recommended)*
     - Welding table with 50 × 50 hole pattern, hole diameter 16 mm
   * - Positioning bolts
     - 16 mm, **4 – 6 pcs**

Supported grippers
------------------

   .. grid-item-card:: Robotiq 2F-140

      :class-card: sd-shadow-sm

      :class-card: sd-shadow-sm

      Supported out of the box.

   The T-piece exists in a **gripper-specific variant**. Download the version
   that matches your hardware from :ref:`p1-tpiece`.

Setup
=====

Step 1 - Print the parts
------------------------

Print the lower and upper section, the workpiece carrier and the T-piece
variant for your gripper (plus the custom gripper fingers if needed).

.. warning::
   Tolerances were tested on a **Bambu Lab X1** - adjust if needed. The fit
   between T-piece and guide should be between a **clearance and a transition
   fit**: it must be possible to insert the T-piece manually with little force.

Step 2 - Assemble the station
-----------------------------

Assemble the lower and upper section using the screws and nuts.

.. figure:: /_images/real_world_tutorial/parkour_1/00_slide_assembly_font.png
   :alt: Assembled station with inserted T-piece
   :width: 90%
   :align: center

   Assembly of the 3D-printed parts with inserted T-piece. **Guide 1** has
   chamfers to simplify insertion, **guide 2** does not.

Step 3 - Clamp everything to the table
--------------------------------------

Clamp the assembled station and the workpiece carrier to your table.

.. figure:: /_images/real_world_tutorial/parkour_1/p1_example_configuration.png
   :alt: Example table configuration
   :width: 90%
   :align: center

   Example configuration, allowing easy access to the T-piece and a minimal
   distance between the T-piece and both guides.

Step 4 - Position the T-piece
-----------------------------

Place the T-piece in the workpiece carrier as shown below.

.. figure:: /_images/real_world_tutorial/parkour_1/t-piece_in_workpiece_carrier.png
   :alt: T-piece seated in the workpiece carrier
   :width: 90%
   :align: center

   T-piece in the workpiece carrier.

Step 5 - Mount the gripper fingers
----------------------------------

If needed, screw the custom 3D-printed gripper fingers onto the Weiss
WSG 50-110 gripper.


How to solve this parkour task
==============================

The task is to **insert and retrieve the T-piece into and out of both guides**,
as shown in the videos below.

.. tab-set::

   .. tab-item:: Guide 1 - chamfered

      .. video:: /_videos/real_world_tutorial/parkour_1/pitasc_realworld_tutorial_p1.mp4
         :width: 100%

         Insertion into and retrieval from guide 1

   .. tab-item:: Guide 2 - not chamfered

      .. video:: /_videos/real_world_tutorial/parkour_1/pitasc_realworld_tutorial_p1_guide2.mp4
         :width: 100%

         Insertion into and retrieval from guide 2

.. admonition:: Why the chamfer matters
   :class: note

   Guides 1 and 2 differ only in the presence or absence of a chamfer at their
   opening. Chamfers simplify automated assembly by allowing the workpiece to
   **self-centre**, which reduces the precision required during assembly.

.. _p1-rough-steps:

Level 1 – Rough steps
---------------------

The sample solution comprises the following rough steps. Each step is
implemented as a skill sequence containing several skills:

#. Pick up the workpiece from the workpiece carrier.
#. Insert the workpiece into **guide 1**.
#. Retrieve the workpiece from **guide 1**.
#. Insert the workpiece into **guide 2**.
#. Retrieve the workpiece from **guide 2**.
#. Place the workpiece back on the workpiece carrier.


.. _p1-example-steps:

Level 2 – Example steps in detail
---------------------------------

.. note::
   For safety reasons, each step begins and ends with the robot moving to its
   home pose by linear movement.

Step 1 - Pick up
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

#. Move to home pose *(position controlled)*
#. Move to a position above the workpiece carrier *(position controlled)*
#. Lower the end effector to the gripping position *(position controlled)*
#. Grip the T-piece *(command to your specific gripper)*
#. Push down on the T-piece to ensure a safe grip
   *(force controlled - only needed for flat gripper fingers)*
#. Move upwards *(position controlled)*
#. Move to home pose *(position controlled)*

Step 2 - Insert into guide 1 *(chamfered)*
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

#. Move to home pose *(position controlled)*
#. Move to a position above the contact position *(position controlled)*
#. Move to a position close to the guide *(position controlled)*
#. Establish contact between T-piece and station *(force controlled)*
#. Slide the T-piece into the guide until it is fully inside
   *(force controlled)*
#. Slide the T-piece until it hits the guide end *(force controlled)*
#. Open the gripper *(command to your specific gripper)*
#. Move to home pose *(position controlled)*

Step 3 - Retrieve from guide 1
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

#. Move to home pose *(position controlled)*
#. Move to a position above the workpiece *(position controlled)*
#. Lower the end effector to the gripping position *(position controlled)*
#. Grip the T-piece *(command to your specific gripper)*
#. Push down on the T-piece to ensure a safe grip
   *(force controlled - only needed for flat gripper fingers)*
#. Slide the T-piece out *(force controlled)*
#. Move up *(position controlled)*
#. Move to home pose *(position controlled)*

Step 4 - Insert into guide 2 *(not chamfered)*
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

#. Move to home pose *(position controlled)*
#. Move to a position above the contact position *(position controlled)*
#. Move to a position close to the guide *(position controlled)*
#. Tilt the T-piece so that the **bottom left corner is exposed**
   *(position controlled)*
#. Establish contact between T-piece and station *(force controlled)*
#. Apply force so that the bottom left corner of the T-piece is pushed into
   the bottom left corner of the guide *(force controlled)*
#. Apply force in the direction of the guide and align the T-piece with the
   guide *(force and position controlled)*
#. Slide the T-piece into the guide *(force controlled)*
#. Open the gripper *(command to your specific gripper)*
#. Move to home pose *(position controlled)*

.. tip::
   Steps 4.4 – 4.7 replace the self-centring that the chamfer provides in
   guide 1. Tilting and corner-seating creates a **known contact state** from
   which the insertion direction is unambiguous.

Step 5 - Retrieve from guide 2
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

#. Move to home pose *(position controlled)*
#. Move to a position above the workpiece *(position controlled)*
#. Lower the end effector to the gripping position *(position controlled)*
#. Grip the T-piece *(command to your specific gripper)*
#. Push down on the T-piece to ensure a safe grip
   *(force controlled - only needed for flat gripper fingers)*
#. Slide the T-piece out *(force controlled)*
#. Move up *(position controlled)*
#. Move to home pose *(position controlled)*

Step 6 - Place in the workpiece carrier
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

#. Move to home pose *(position controlled)*
#. Move to a position above the workpiece carrier *(position controlled)*
#. Establish contact between T-piece and workpiece carrier
   *(force controlled)*
#. Open the gripper *(command to your specific gripper)*
#. Move upwards *(position controlled)*


.. _p1-tips:

Level 3 – Tips on using skills
------------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 35 65

   * - Goal
     - Recommended approach
   * - Establish contact with the surroundings
     - Use a **guarded approach**.
   * - Predictable linear motion with minimal jerk
     - Use ``lin_time_optimal_ruckig``.
   * - Follow a wall or guide under constant force
     - Use the pitasc **slide** skill.
   * - End a skill on a condition
     - Attach **monitors** to react to a force threshold or travelled distance.

.. danger::
   **Tare the force/torque sensor before every force-controlled skill.**
   This is the single most important step for reliable real-world operation -
   an untared sensor makes the measured contact force meaningless and can
   damage the workpiece or the station. See the code snippets below for how
   taring is invoked in practice.

.. tip::
   Add **user confirms** between skills. This makes it much easier to spot
   problems and abort execution safely while developing.

--------------------------------------

Preamble
~~~~~~~~

The preamble handles all imports of relevant models and configures the
application environment, in which every component (robots, sensors, actuators)
must be specified in order to be controlled later on. It also defines a boolean
parameter to activate or deactivate all user confirmations at once.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The pose at which the gripper held the inserted workpiece was saved. The

Step 4: Insert into guide 2
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

While inserting into guide 2 appears to be the same task as guide 1, the lack
of a chamfer proves to be an obstacle. The T-piece is therefore **tilted and
pushed into one corner** of the guide to achieve a form of mutual centring.

Step 5: Retrieve from guide 2
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Similar to the retrieval from guide 1. The main difference is the **change of
direction** for sliding and for moving the T-piece away from the guide.

.. dropdown:: ``5_retrieve_guide_2.xml``
   :icon: code
   :color: light

   .. literalinclude:: /_xml/real_world_tutorial/5_retrieve_guide_2.xml

Step 6: Place in the workpiece carrier
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The final step places the T-piece back in the carrier. To prevent an unwanted
collision in case the workpiece slipped during retrieval, the robot first moves
to a position just above the carrier, then gently establishes contact and
pushes the workpiece into the recess.
